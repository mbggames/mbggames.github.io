MBG Puzzles · Word Puzzle - where the word lists come from

The files in this folder and www/js/word/words-embedded.js are lists of words selected
by tools/build_vocab.py from these sources. Each list keeps the licence of its source.

  ENABLE word list - public domain.
  english-words by dwyl (github.com/dwyl/english-words) - The Unlicense (public domain).

Korean (ko-dict.txt, ko-extra.txt, Korean everyday words) - version 1.11
  hunspell-dict-ko, the Korean spelling dictionary (github.com/spellcheck-ko/hunspell-dict-ko),
  whose words come from 한국어기초사전 (Basic Korean Dictionary) of the National Institute of Korean
  Language (krdict.korean.go.kr) - Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 2.0 Korea - and from
  표준국어대사전 / 우리말샘; the word data of hunspell-dict-ko is CC BY-SA 4.0. The Korean word lists
  are therefore distributed under the Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 licence
  (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). Secret words (everyday and "All words") are
  only nouns, pronouns, numerals, adverbs, determiners and interjections of 한국어기초사전 - no
  names of people, companies or parties.
  Korean frequency and accepted-word lists supplied by MBG (ko_KR easy / medium / hard,
  accepted words, headwords): used to decide which dictionary words are everyday words, and as
  more accepted guesses.
  Korean is written in these files in the letters of the Korean keyboard (풀어쓰기, one letter
  per key of the 두벌식 keyboard: 사과 -> ㅅㅏㄱㅗㅏ), and so are the everyday words in
  www/js/word/words-embedded.js. Built by tools/build_ko_words.py.

Accents: in Spanish, French and Portuguese the lists are written without accents (árbol ->
arbol, école -> ecole, ação -> acao), as the game plays them; German keeps ä, ö, ü and ß,
Turkish and Polish their own letters. Arabic is written without short vowels and with
alif instead of alif with hamza or madda (أ إ آ -> ا); ء ؤ ئ ة ى stay letters.

Words that are never secret words (rude words, slurs, drugs, violence) were chosen by hand,
helped by the List of Dirty, Naughty, Obscene and Otherwise Bad Words (LDNOOBW,
github.com/LDNOOBW, CC BY 4.0); none of its words are included in the lists (Korean: in no list
at all, not even as an accepted guess).

Choice of everyday (common) words
  FrequencyWords by Hermit Dave (github.com/hermitdave/FrequencyWords), built from the
  OpenSubtitles 2018 corpus - content CC BY-SA 4.0. Used only to decide which dictionary
  words are common; the frequency lists themselves are not included.
