Privacy Policy

MBG Puzzles (Codebreaker and Word Puzzle) · Effective 12 October 2026

In short

MBG Puzzles has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no chat. What you type and your scores stay on your phone – unless you choose to join the world top 20 (see below), which shows your player name, country and best scores to other players. The free edition has no purchases at all; in the edition with a subscription and hint credits, payment is handled entirely by Google Play.

Who we are

What stays on your phone

To work, the app stores the following only in its own storage on your device:

  • user names you type to keep your own high score, and your best scores;
  • your settings: language, background, text size, sound and number or word length;
  • a game in progress, so you can continue it later;
  • hint credits for each player name: free credits used today and this week, credits earned with high scores or bought;
  • statistics of your games for each player name: games played and won, streaks, tries and fastest times;
  • your results of the puzzles of the day (the number and the word of the day), so that each can be played only once and your daily streak can be counted. The puzzles of the day are worked out on your phone from the date; nothing is sent to get them;
  • only in an edition with a subscription: the date you first opened the app (for the free week), when you last used each daily free game, and whether a subscription is active.

This data is not sent to us or to anyone else (except what you send to the world list, below). It is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its storage. If Android backup is turned on (Google account), Android includes this data in your phone’s backup with Google and brings it back when you reinstall the app or set up a new phone with the same Google account. The backup is made and kept by Google under its own privacy policy; you control it in your phone’s settings.

Moving to a new phone

In a game, Settings › Move to a new phone shows a code that holds your player names, best scores, statistics, results of the puzzles of the day and settings – and, for names on the world list, the key that keeps those names yours. The code is made on your phone and is never sent to us: you pass it on yourself (for example to yourself by WhatsApp or e-mail) and enter it on the new phone. Anyone with the code could take over this data, so keep it to yourself. Hint credits and your plan are not in the code.

World top 20 (only if you join)

The world list is optional and only for players aged 13 and over. Each player name joins on its own: logged in with that name, you choose “Join the world list”, enter your year of birth (it stays on your phone and is used only to check the age) and agree to the rules. Then, each time you win with that name, the app sends to our server: the player name, the game, the number or word length, the language, the number of tries and seconds, and a random code made on your phone for this installation (it is not linked to your name, phone or Google account). The server works out the score and notes the country of your internet connection – only the country, never the address. Your player name, country and best scores are visible to everyone who opens the world list.

To stop abuse, the server briefly counts requests per connection using a coded value of the network address; these counters are deleted after two hours. Players on the world list can report names (a report is stored with the reporting installation’s random code, so that each player counts only once); names that break the rules are hidden. The server is run for us by Cloudflare, Inc. (Cloudflare Workers and D1), which processes the data on our behalf.

Scores stay on the world list until you remove them: logged in with your player name, Settings › World list › Off deletes all scores of that name from the server at once. If you uninstall the app or clear its storage first, the app can no longer prove that the scores are yours – then write to us at the contact address with the player name, and we delete them.

So that the list stays fast with many players, a win is sent only when it beats that name’s best of the week (same game, length and language). A win of a puzzle of the day is sent once, with the number of that day’s puzzle, and appears on that day’s list.

Subscriptions and payments (only where offered)

Subscriptions and hint credits are sold and processed by Google Play. We never see your card or payment details. To unlock unlimited play, the app asks Google Play on your phone whether your Google account has an active subscription. Google’s Privacy Policy applies to payments.

Google gives us order reports (for example order number, plan, price, date and country). We use them only for accounting, tax and to answer your support requests.

Children and families

MBG Puzzles is made for players of all ages, including children. Children under 13 cannot join the world list, and nothing about them leaves the phone: no names, e-mail addresses, location, contacts or device identifiers. There are no ads, no chat and no links to social networks; the only links out of the app are this policy and the contact buttons (WhatsApp, e-mail) for writing to us. A name typed for the high score list stays on the phone; players can simply use a first name or a nickname.

Your choices

  • Delete the app’s data: Android Settings › Apps › MBG Puzzles › Storage › Clear storage.
  • Cancel a subscription (where offered): Google Play › Payments & subscriptions › Subscriptions.

لمنع إساءة الاستخدام، يعدّ الخادم لفترة قصيرة الطلبات الواردة من كل اتصال باستخدام قيمة مشفّرة لعنوان الشبكة؛ وتُحذف هذه العدادات بعد ساعتين. يمكن للاعبين في القائمة العالمية الإبلاغ عن الأسماء (يُحفظ البلاغ مع الرمز العشوائي للتثبيت الذي أبلغ، حتى يُحتسب كل لاعب مرة واحدة فقط)؛ وتُخفى الأسماء المخالفة للقواعد. تشغّل الخادمَ لصالحنا شركة Cloudflare, Inc.‎ (Cloudflare Workers وD1)، وهي تعالج البيانات نيابة عنا.

تبقى النتائج في القائمة العالمية حتى تحذفها: بعد الدخول باسم لاعبك، اختر الإعدادات › القائمة العالمية › إيقاف، فتُحذف جميع نتائج هذا الاسم من الخادم دفعة واحدة. إذا ألغيت تثبيت التطبيق أو مسحت مساحة تخزينه قبل ذلك، فلن يستطيع التطبيق إثبات أن النتائج تخصك – عندها راسلنا على عنوان التواصل مع ذكر اسم اللاعب، وسنحذفها.

لتبقى القائمة سريعة مع عدد كبير من اللاعبين، لا يُرسل الفوز إلا إذا تجاوز أفضل نتيجة لذلك الاسم هذا الأسبوع (اللعبة نفسها والطول نفسه واللغة نفسها). أما الفوز في لغز اليوم فيُرسل مرة واحدة مع رقم لغز ذلك اليوم، ويظهر في قائمة ذلك اليوم.

الاشتراكات والمدفوعات (فقط حيث تُعرض)

تبيع Google Play الاشتراكات ورصيد التلميحات وتعالجها. لا نرى أبدًا بيانات بطاقتك أو الدفع. لفتح اللعب بلا حدود، يسأل التطبيقُ Google Play على هاتفك عمّا إذا كان لحساب Google الخاص بك اشتراك فعّال. وتنطبق سياسة خصوصية Google على المدفوعات.

ترسل لنا Google تقارير الطلبات (مثل رقم الطلب والخطة والسعر والتاريخ والبلد). ونستخدمها فقط للمحاسبة والضرائب والرد على طلبات الدعم منك.

الأطفال والعائلات

صُنع MBG Puzzles للاعبين من كل الأعمار، بمن فيهم الأطفال. لا يمكن للأطفال دون 13 عامًا الانضمام إلى القائمة العالمية، ولا يغادر الهاتفَ أي شيء عنهم: لا أسماء ولا عناوين بريد إلكتروني ولا موقع ولا جهات اتصال ولا معرّفات للجهاز. لا توجد إعلانات ولا دردشة ولا روابط إلى الشبكات الاجتماعية؛ والروابط الوحيدة إلى خارج التطبيق هي هذه السياسة وأزرار التواصل (واتساب، البريد الإلكتروني) لمراسلتنا. الاسم الذي يُكتب لقائمة أفضل النتائج يبقى على الهاتف؛ ويمكن للاعبين استخدام الاسم الأول فقط أو لقب.
